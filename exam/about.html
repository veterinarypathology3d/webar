Project Overview

This web-based viewer was developed to provide students and instructors with easy access to digital histology and whole-slide image resources. The platform uses OpenSeadragon to display high-resolution Zoomify tile images directly in the browser.

The goal is to support observation, tissue recognition, lesion description, and visual learning through an interactive, zoomable slide environment.

Educational Purpose

  • Provide browser-based access to high-resolution histology or pathology slides.
  • Help learners practice systematic visual observation and tissue recognition.
  • Support integration of anatomy, histology, pathology, and clinical reasoning.
